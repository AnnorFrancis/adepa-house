What is included

Braids, weaves and treatments include a wash. Extensions are included where the card says so. Wigs and bundles are in our shop, or bring your own.

Booking and walk-ins

Walk in any time and take the next free chair. If you book, your chair is held for fifteen minutes past the hour. There is no deposit.

How to pay

Services are paid at the desk when you are done: cash, mobile money or card. Tips go straight to the person who did your hair.
