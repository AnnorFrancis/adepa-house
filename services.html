The menu.

Every treatment, its price and how long it takes. Add as many as you like, for yourself or the whole family, and reserve them together.

The Salon

Consultation first

Every visit starts with a few minutes with your stylist. If your hair needs more time or product than the menu allows, we tell you the price before we begin.

Reservations

Your chair is held for fifteen minutes past your time. Change or cancel free of charge up to twenty-four hours before.

Settling up

Treatments are settled at the house by card, mobile money or cash. Gratuity is at your discretion and goes directly to your artist.
