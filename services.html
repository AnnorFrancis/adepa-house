What you see here is what you pay at the desk. If your hair needs more time or more product than usual, we tell you before we start, not after.

What is included

Braids, weaves and treatments include a wash. Extensions are included where the list says so. Wig and weave hair is yours to bring, or ask what we have in stock.

Booking and walk-ins

Walk in any time and take the next free chair. If you book, your chair is held for fifteen minutes past the hour. There is no deposit.

How to pay

Cash, mobile money or card, at the desk when you are done. Tips go straight to the person who did your hair.
