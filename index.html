Inside
the house.

What a visit looks like, before you make one.

Twelve chairs in one room

Barbers on one side, stylists on the other, and a kids’ corner by the window where you can keep an eye on them.

Two braiders on every long style

Four hands instead of two, so a style that takes six hours elsewhere takes about four here.

A new blade for every client

Razors are opened in front of you. Clippers are disinfected between cuts, and the barber wears gloves.

A nail desk that stays open late

Come after work. The last nail appointment of the day starts at seven.

Booked clients sit first

Walk in whenever you like and take the next free chair. If you have booked, your chair is waiting.
