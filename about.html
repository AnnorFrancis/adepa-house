About us.

We built the studio we wanted for our own families: one address where everyone is looked after properly, on the same afternoon.

Why one studio
for the whole family.

Most families in Accra keep three appointments for hair: a barber for him, a braider for her and somewhere patient enough for the children. That is three drives, three waits and most of a Saturday.

Nakus Beauty Studio brings women’s hair, men’s grooming, kids’ hair, and nails and beauty under one roof, with senior stylists in each. You arrive together, you are looked after at the same time, and you leave together.
