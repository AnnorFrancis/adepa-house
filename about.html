The house.

In Twi, adepa means a good thing, a thing of value. We built the house we wanted for our own families: one address where everyone is looked after properly, on the same afternoon.

Why one house
for the whole family.

Most families in Accra keep three appointments for hair: a barber for him, a braider for her and somewhere patient enough for the children. That is three drives, three waits and most of a Saturday.

Adepa House brings a salon, a grooming room, a children’s suite and a beauty room under one roof, with senior artists in each. You arrive together, you are looked after at the same time, and you leave together.

The Little Suite.
